Effective: 2026-11-25. App: Ant Tooth Black (com.haomao.mayiyahei), published by 好猫(天津)科技有限公司 (Haomao (Tianjin) Technology Co., Ltd.). Contact: haomaotj002@163.com.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, player name and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. The game has no account system, and we do not upload this data to our own servers. We do not collect your phone number, contacts, precise location or microphone content.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos, and occasional full-screen ads when you return to the home screen after winning a few levels (never during play). AdMob may use the advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions, we ask for your consent when you first open the app (Google User Messaging Platform), and you can change your choice at any time in Menu > Ad Privacy Options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. See Google's advertising policies.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details. Purchase records and subscription status are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat (RevenueCat privacy policy) so that we can unlock what you bought, restore it on a new phone and verify subscriptions. RevenueCat identifies you with a random app user ID, not your name or email. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancel any time in Google Play > Subscriptions. Coins, items and vouchers are virtual items with no cash value and are not refundable except where the law requires.

Analytics and crash logs (Firebase)

To fix crashes and improve the game we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect anonymous usage events, crash stack traces and device information (model, Android version, language, app version) and an app-instance ID. They do not contain your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy information.

Sharing a picture

When you tap "Share Score" the game creates a picture on your device and opens the system share sheet. You decide where it goes. "Save Image" stores the picture in your gallery (Android may ask for a permission on older versions).

Children

The game is a casual puzzle game suitable for players aged 12 and over (younger players should play with a guardian). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are restricted to the "G" content rating.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone.
  • Change ad consent: Menu > Ad Privacy Options (where shown), or Android settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • To ask about, access or delete data held by RevenueCat or Firebase for your device, email us at haomaotj002@163.com and tell us your app user ID (shown to you on request) or the device details.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and the in-app text.

保存在你手机里的内容

游戏进度、昵称和设置只保存在你手机的应用本地存储里。游戏没有账号系统，我们不会把这些数据上传到自己的服务器，也不收集手机号、通讯录、精确位置和麦克风内容。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏通过 Google AdMob 展示广告：自愿观看的激励视频，以及你赢下几关后回到主页时偶尔出现的全屏广告（战斗中不会出现）。AdMob 可能使用广告标识符和设备信息来展示和统计广告。在欧洲经济区、英国等地区，首次打开应用时会先征求你的同意（Google UMP），之后可随时在「菜单 > 广告隐私选项」里修改；你也可以在 Android 设置里重置或删除广告标识符。广告内容限制为适合所有年龄。

购买与订阅

购买由 Google Play 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡信息。购买记录和订阅状态由 Google Play 和 RevenueCat 处理，用于发放你买到的内容、在新手机上恢复购买和核验订阅；RevenueCat 用随机的应用用户 ID 识别你，不含姓名或邮箱。订阅会自动续订直到取消，可随时在 Google Play「订阅」里取消。金币、道具和券是没有现金价值的虚拟物品，除法律另有规定外购买后不退。

统计与崩溃日志（Firebase）

为了修复闪退、改进游戏，我们使用 Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics，收集匿名的使用事件、崩溃堆栈和设备信息（型号、系统版本、语言、应用版本）以及应用实例 ID，不含你的姓名或联系方式。

分享图片

点「晒战绩」时，游戏在你的手机上生成一张图片并打开系统分享面板，发给谁由你决定；「保存图片」会把图片存到相册（旧版本 Android 可能请求相册权限）。

未成年人

本游戏是休闲益智游戏，适合 12 岁及以上玩家（更小的玩家请在监护人陪同下游戏）。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息，广告内容限制为「G」级。

你的选择

卸载应用会删除保存在手机里的数据；广告同意可在「菜单 > 广告隐私选项」或 Android 设置里修改；如需查询或删除 RevenueCat、Firebase 中与你设备相关的数据，请发邮件到 haomaotj002@163.com。
